Interactive 3D breadboard. Arrow keys turn it, plus and minus zoom, zero resets the view, space presses the red button, P toggles power, A turns it automatically, L toggles labels, N and Shift N step through the parts, Enter holds the button down or opens the part mover, square brackets change tour step, question mark lists every key.
